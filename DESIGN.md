## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an authoritative, institutional, yet progressive aesthetic tailored for professional corporate training, leadership development, and strategic consultancy in Southeast Asia. The system projects confidence, enterprise-grade reliability, and dynamic capability.

The design movement synthesizes **Corporate / Modern** precision with architectural clarity:
- Clean geometric structure reinforced by refined hairline dividers and micro-elevations.
- Purposeful contrast: resolute deep navy blue commands institutional authority, while precise warm amber/gold accents highlight transformation, growth, and key executive actions.
- Professional clarity without coldness; warm slate neutrals foster an environment optimized for dense course catalogs, corporate certifications, trainer rosters, and bilingual (Bahasa Malaysia / English) operational fluency.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative deep maritime tones against warm luminous highlights and slate neutrals.

- **Primary (`#0B2545`)**: Deep Navy Blue. Used for executive navigation bars, core structural framing, primary typography, high-priority actions, and definitive brand touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Radiant Amber. Used intentionally for focal metrics, HRD Corp claimable badges, certification highlights, primary callouts, and key active states.
- **Tertiary (`#133E87`)**: Vibrant Royal Navy. Provides tonal depth for interactive hover states, secondary indicators, active tab underlines, and functional metadata groupings.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Slate. Scales from base background canvas (`#F8FAFC`), card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), borders (`#E2E8F0`), to secondary body and caption labels (`#475569` and `#1E293B`).

Functional semantic colors:
- **Success**: `#059669` (HRD Corp Approved / Verified).
- **Warning**: `#D97706` (Seat Limit Approaching / Pending Approval).
- **Critical**: `#DC2626` (Session Closed / Expired).
- **Surface Tint**: `#F1F5F9` (Subtle track panels and bilingual toggle frames).

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural display and section headers with **Inter** for readable body and utility text.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Delivers contemporary executive polish without sterility. Its wide aperture and balanced geometric curves create clean hero titles, course module headings, and card titles.
- **Inter**: Ensures data legibility across multi-lingual interfaces, registration tables, program schedules, and analytical reporting metrics.

Usage Rules:
- All display headers use tight negative letter-spacing to command visual presence.
- Badges, status markers, and bilingual toggles utilize `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight uppercase tracking to maintain legibility at small scales.
- Paragraph text defaults to `body-md` in slate `#334155` for optimal reading contrast against `#F8FAFC` and white backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by strict 4px/8px micro-increments.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1280px` centered, `2rem` (32px) page margin, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, `1.5rem` (24px) margin, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Complex training schedules collapse into horizontal scroll containers or stacked accordions.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, `1rem` (16px) margin, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Actions, metrics, and bilingual switchers pin to full-width or segmented compact rows.

Component spacing rhythm:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge internals, icon-to-text labels, and inline tag clusters.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, form field gaps, and list groupings.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding, module dividers, and modal headers.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for layout section vertical spacing.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this design system uses sharp, low-blur corporate shadow framing paired with crisp hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). This creates structured, high-trust digital paper layers rather than dramatic, diffuse floating elements.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Background `#F8FAFC` and sunken interactive inputs. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Shelves)**: Background `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Action Bars)**: Background `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.06)`. Subtle transition: `transform: translateY(-2px)`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns & Modals)**: Background `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #94A3B8`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)`.
- **Accent Elevation (Gold Focus / Selected Card)**: Outer glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.

## Shapes
The design system uses a controlled **Soft (`1`)** shape language to reinforce structure, precision, and corporate discipline.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards)**: `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius. This gives components a tailored edge without looking harsh.
- **Containers & Modal Shells (`rounded-lg`)**: `0.5rem` (8px). Keeps enterprise dialogs, tables, and corporate dashboards stable and architecturally grounded.
- **Status Pills & Micro Indicators**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) reserved specifically for HRD Corp status badges, bilingual segment toggles, and live availability pills. This contrast between crisp rectangular cards and soft pill badges immediately directs the eye to critical metadata.
